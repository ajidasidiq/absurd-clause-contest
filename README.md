Entry for the make.fun bounty "The Absurd Clause Contest": one absurd
clause hidden deep inside a terms-of-service agreement, 200 characters
or fewer.

## The entry

```
Section 12.4: you hereby grant the Company a perpetual, royalty-free licence to your dreams, including the right to sublicense any recurring nightmares to third parties without further notice.
```

## Run

```
python3 main.py
```

Writes the clause to `/output/entry.txt` (192 characters, one line).

## Why this line

It is shaped like a real clause (numbered section, "hereby grant",
"perpetual, royalty-free licence", "without further notice"), so it
reads as genuine legal boilerplate until the absurdity hits: the user
licenses away their dreams, and the Company may sublicense the
recurring nightmares to third parties. The joke lands on one reading
with no setup, and the image is concrete — your nightmares, resold.
